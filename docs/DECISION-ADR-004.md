# ADR-004 — Separate onboarding from client work

## Decision

The course begins with a two-part DataScope employee-training sequence before students receive their first client file.

## Context

The introductory material covers professional transition, lawful intelligence research, ethics, source types and source evaluation. These are foundational competencies rather than client-specific work.

## Consequences

- The homepage leads to an Academy dashboard.
- Introductory content uses `TRAIN-*` identifiers.
- Client cases begin only after completion of onboarding.
- Moodle remains the location for formal assignment submission and grading.
